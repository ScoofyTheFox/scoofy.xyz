✦ reqwerty translator ✦

Type something and convert it using your alphabet. Spaces stay as spaces; underscores also work.

✦ spaces, numbers & an example ✦

Spaces stay spaces. Numbers swap too, so the translator can do the whole message:

0 ↔ 11 ↔ 0 2 ↔ 93 ↔ 8 4 ↔ 75 ↔ 6 6 ↔ 57 ↔ 4 8 ↔ 39 ↔ 2

hello → fiaaw

Example using the key: H → F, E → I, L → A, L → A, O → W.
