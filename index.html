How to play Dig with left click, place with right click. Sell ores to Bramble (interact key, default F) for Scoofcy, then buy gear, snacks, hats, outfits and baubles. E opens your inventory with your fursona and equipment beside it. Eat to keep your food bar up; hold Ctrl to sprint. Explore biomes and the ocean, find ruins, pyramids, igloos and shipwrecks, and dig down through deepslate for rubies, amethyst and mythril. Use the PawOS computer at the stall for info and fun. F3 shows debug info, B shows hitboxes, F5 changes camera. Everything is rebindable in Settings.
Autosaves in your browser — or run the included server.py to save into a data/ folder.
